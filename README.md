## Understand the certification and version

HCIA-Storage is Huawei Certified ICT Associate-Storage. It focuses on basic storage deployment, operations and maintenance, and management capabilities. The intended audience includes Huawei engineers, channel engineers, college students, and ICT practitioners.

Huawei lists the official exam code as H13-611. The H13-611_V5.0 label identifies the version used for this study topic; it should not be confused with a different official code.

The verified examination information includes:

- Exam name: HCIA-Storage.
- Version: V5.0.
- Assessment: written examination.
- Duration: 90 minutes.
- Passing score: 600 out of 1,000.
- Listed fee: 200 USD.
- Languages: Chinese and English.

Candidates should have basic knowledge of networks, computer components, and Windows or Linux operating systems. Those knowledge expectations should guide your starting point before you spend time on storage-specific details.

## Read the weighting before planning your study

Basic Storage Technologies accounts for 50% of the blueprint. Huawei Intelligent Storage Products and Features contributes 35%, Storage System O&M Management 10%, and Storage Technology Trends 5%.

These percentages describe domain emphasis, not a fixed number of questions. They suggest spending most preparation time on foundations and product capabilities while still reviewing the complete syllabus.

The official guide also states that related content beyond the listed points may appear. Use its topics to organize learning, but avoid treating a short objective label as a complete technical explanation.

## Connect the ten listed knowledge points

The trends domain contains Storage Technology Trends. Use it to establish context for the rest of the material rather than collecting unsupported predictions about the industry.

The basic technologies domain includes Intelligent Data Storage System, RAID Technologies, Common Storage Protocols, and Storage Network Architecture. Study these as connected topics. When reviewing a diagram, identify what the components do and how the network and protocol choices relate to access.

The Huawei products domain covers Huawei Intelligent Storage Products, Storage System Operation Management, Storage Resource Tuning Technologies and Applications, and Storage Data Protection Technologies and Applications.

Storage System O&M Management is also a separate weighted domain. Preserve that distinction in your notes instead of assuming that every management topic belongs to the same section.

## Use official learning material actively

Huawei recommends HCIA-Storage V5.0 Courses and HCIA-Storage V5.0 Training. Begin with the sections that match the largest domain, then use the product and operational material to place the underlying technologies in context.

For each topic, write a definition and one question you still need to resolve. A note that says only “review RAID” is difficult to act on. A note that identifies which part of the explanation you cannot follow gives you a clear next step.

Where you have suitable training access, observe the documented workflow in a learning environment. Explain the purpose of a step before performing it. Follow the applicable lab instructions rather than experimenting on a production system.

## Make practice reveal gaps

Start with topic-focused sessions. When an answer is wrong, decide whether the problem was unfamiliar terminology, a misunderstood relationship, or a missed condition in the question.

Review correct guesses as well. Recognizing an answer from an earlier attempt does not establish that you can explain the concept without the choices.

Once the individual topics feel clearer, use mixed revision to check whether you can distinguish a foundational concept from a product feature or an O&M task. Keep the emphasis on explaining why an answer fits.

## Put the credential in a practical context

HCIA-Storage gives beginning storage professionals a structured way to demonstrate foundational knowledge. Its focus on basic deployment, O&M, and management makes the study relevant to candidates preparing for storage engineering and IT technical support roles.

The credential does not remove the need for practical experience. It provides a defined body of knowledge around which that experience can develop.

A sensible final sequence is to revisit weak foundations, review the Huawei feature objectives, and then complete mixed practice. Before scheduling, check Huawei's current certification information for the applicable examination arrangements. Use your final revision session to explain the topics you once found confusing, not simply to repeat questions you already recognize.
